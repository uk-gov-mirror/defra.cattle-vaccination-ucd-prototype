{# ---- V5 ---- #}

Streamlined skin test reporting

V5

Experimental

An experimental fork of V4 that streamlines the end of the skin test journey. Everything up to the reactor step is the same as V4, and the "did any cattle react?" and "were all cattle tested?" questions are gone from both variants.

{# Two ways of asking the same question, built to be compared. They write identical data, so everything downstream - check your answers, the confirmation, the report - cannot tell them apart. The interaction is the only variable. #}

Three journeys to compare. They record the same thing, on the same 102 cattle, and end in the same place – only the way the vet gets there differs. V4 is the baseline the two V5 variants are being measured against.

Table

  • one review table accounts for the whole herd, instead of three separate passes
  • every animal carries a result: clear, reaction or not tested
  • a quick-apply block sets a result for the whole herd at once, so only the exceptions are changed after it
  • measurements and not-tested reasons are recorded inline, on the same screen

Add another

  • the herd is never listed – the vet names only the exceptions
  • search an ear tag, record the reading, add another
  • untested animals and their reasons are on a screen of their own
  • anything not named is reported as tested with no reaction

Three passes (V4)

  • the existing journey, unchanged, on the same herd as the two above
  • tick the cattle that reacted, then enter their readings
  • then a second pass for the cattle that were not tested, and their reasons
  • anything left untouched is reported as tested with no reaction

These are part journeys. All of them start with Mill House Farm and a SICCT test already loaded, so there is nothing to set up before you begin.

Vaccination data in Salesforce – prototype

V1

Business-need led

Explores how vaccination data is searched, checked, edited and viewed in Salesforce. This is based on a business need, not a user need — it has not yet been started from, or validated against, researched user needs. What's in the prototype:

  • Multi ear tag search — look up several animals at once by entering more than one ear tag number.
  • Vax checker — check the vaccination status of an animal or herd.
  • Edit vax data — amend or correct recorded vaccination details.
  • Vax history — view the vaccination history for an animal or herd.
